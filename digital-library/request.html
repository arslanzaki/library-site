Book Request Form

Can't find a book in our collection? Request it here and we'll do our best to add it to our library. Please provide accurate information to help us process your request efficiently.

Student ID is optional but helps us process your request faster

What Happens Next?

1

Request Review

Our library staff will review your request within 2-3 business days.

2

Availability Check

We'll check if the book is available from publishers or other libraries.

3

Notification

You'll receive an email notification about the status of your request.

4

Ready for Pickup

If approved, the book will be available for pickup within 1-2 weeks.
