Library Rules & Regulations

Please review and follow these important rules to ensure a pleasant and productive environment for all library users.

1

No Food or Drinks Allowed

To protect our books and maintain a clean environment, please do not bring any food, beverages, or gum into the library. Water bottles with secure lids are permitted in designated areas only.

2

Return Books Within 14 Days

All borrowed materials must be returned within 14 days of checkout. Late returns may result in fines and temporary suspension of borrowing privileges. Renewals can be requested in person or online.

3

Maintain Library Silence

Please keep noise to a minimum throughout the library. Designated areas are available for group study and discussions. Use headphones for audio content and keep phone calls brief or use the lobby area.

4

Handle Books with Care

Treat all library materials with respect. Do not write, highlight, or damage books. Report any damaged materials immediately. Place books carefully on tables and shelves to prevent damage.

5

Maximum 10 Books Per Person

To ensure fair access for all students, each person may borrow a maximum of 10 books at any given time. Special arrangements can be made for research projects with prior approval.

6

Student ID Required

A valid university student ID card is required to borrow books and access certain library services. ID cards are non-transferable and must be presented upon request by library staff.

7

Computer Usage Policy

Library computers are available for academic research and assignments only. Gaming, streaming, and non-academic activities are prohibited. Time limits may apply during peak hours.

8

Reserve Study Rooms in Advance

Group study rooms can be reserved up to 24 hours in advance through our online booking system or at the circulation desk. Rooms are available for 2-hour sessions maximum.

9

Lost or Damaged Book Fees

Borrowers are responsible for the full replacement cost of lost or damaged books, plus a $5 processing fee. Report lost books immediately to avoid additional late fees.

10

Respect Library Property

Keep the library clean and organized. Return books to their proper locations using our automated sorting system. Report any equipment malfunctions or safety hazards to staff immediately.

Questions About These Rules?

If you have any questions about library rules or need clarification, please don't hesitate to ask our friendly staff at the circulation desk or contact us during library hours.
